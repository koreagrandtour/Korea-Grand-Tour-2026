Non-negotiables

Look after the road.
Look after each other.

Korea Grand Tour is a touring event. It is not a race.

01

This is not a race

Obey Korean traffic law, posted limits and all team instructions. Never compete for position or arrival time.

02

Zero alcohol and driving

Alcohol and driving are completely incompatible with the event and result in immediate removal.

03

Your car, your responsibility

Keep it road-legal, insured and mechanically fit. You remain responsible for your vehicle, passengers and decisions.

04

Give space

Keep safe following distance and never block roads, residents, junctions or emergency access while regrouping.

05

If separated

Continue safely using navigation. Never make a risky manoeuvre to rejoin. Contact the team only when parked safely.

06

If something happens

Protect people first, stop somewhere safe, call emergency services when needed, then notify the team.

How to use this roadbook

Your route at a glance.

Each day shows target arrival and departure times. Tap any location to open it in Kakao Maps. Live instructions from the Korea Grand Tour Team always take priority over this schedule.

01
Arrive ready

Fuelled, rested and at the morning meeting point before the stated departure time.

02
Follow the road, not the car ahead

Use navigation and drive within your own safe limits. Never rush to close a gap.

03
Stay reachable

Keep the official group communication channel available for live changes and regroup instructions.

04
Timing is a target

Traffic, weather and group movement may change the plan. The team will confirm adjustments.

Participant essentials

The road works when we look after one another.

01

This is not a race

Obey Korean traffic law, posted limits and all instructions. Never compete for position, speed or arrival time.

02

Zero alcohol and driving

Alcohol and driving are completely incompatible with the event and result in immediate removal.

03

Your car, your responsibility

Keep your vehicle road-legal, insured and mechanically fit. Bring fuel, fluids and anything your car uniquely needs.

04

Give space

Maintain safe following distance. Do not block roads, junctions, residents or access points while regrouping.

05

If separated

Continue safely using Kakao navigation. Do not make risky manoeuvres to rejoin. Contact the team when parked safely.

06

If something happens

Stop somewhere safe, protect people first, contact emergency services when needed, then notify the Korea Grand Tour Team.
